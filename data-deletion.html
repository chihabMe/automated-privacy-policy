Data Deletion Instructions

You can request deletion of personal information associated with your interaction with Automated's WhatsApp or Instagram automation.

How to request deletion

  1. Email chihab.mg.me@gmail.com with the subject line Data Deletion Request.
  2. Include the WhatsApp phone number or Instagram username associated with your request.
  3. We may request reasonable information to verify that the request relates to your account before completing it.

What happens next

We will review the request and delete applicable information that we control, unless we must retain it for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or legitimate operational reasons. We will confirm the outcome by email.

Meta information

For information held directly by Meta, you may also use the privacy and account-management controls provided by WhatsApp, Instagram, or Facebook.

For our general privacy practices, see the Privacy Policy.
